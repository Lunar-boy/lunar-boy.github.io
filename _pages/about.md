I am **Ellis C. Wu**, an M.Sc. student in
**Computational Modeling and Simulation** at
**Technische Universität Dresden**, specializing
in Visual Computing.

My research interests lie at the intersection of
**Artificial Intelligence Theory, Neural Network
Architectures, and Efficient Computing Systems**.

## Research Interests

- **AI Theory:** Generalization, reasoning, and representation learning.
- **Neural Architectures:** Transformers,
  State Space Models, and efficient attention.
- **AI Systems:** High-performance computing,
  GPU acceleration, and distributed training.
- **Physical AI:** World Action models and vision-language-action models.

## Research Projects

### How Much Can Language Models Memorize and Reason?
*Work in Progress*

Developing an information-theoretic framework to distinguish memorization capacity from generalizable reasoning using random sequences and synthetic graph tasks.


### Gene2Image: Pathway-Guided Histopathology Generation
*Work in Progress*

Developing a pathway-guided Transformer encoder with learnable sparse gene-to-pathway mappings to condition a Rectified
Flow model for generating histopathology images from single-cell RNA expression.

### E-Nose Gas Classification and H2 S Regression
*Work in Progress*

Developed ML pipelines for eight-class gas recognition and H2S concentration prediction from multivariate time series collected by a 16-sensor chip.

### ROS 2 Adaptive Robotic Assembly
*Work in Progress*

Developed a ROS 2 robotic manipulation framework for Franka Panda, integrating MoveIt 2, Gazebo, and ros2 control for
physics-based pick-and-place simulation.
